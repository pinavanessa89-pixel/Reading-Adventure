👩‍👧 Grown-Up Corner

Use this low-pressure 5-10 minute check to find the right starting point. Do not teach or correct during the check; just notice what she can do independently.

1. Letter sounds

Show m, s, t, p, n, a, i one at a time. Ask: What sound does this letter make?

2. Beginning sounds

Say sun, map, fish. Ask: What sound do you hear first?

3. Ending sounds

Say map, sun, cat. Ask: What sound do you hear last?

4. Blending

Say slowly: /m/ /a/ /p/, /s/ /a/ /t/, /p/ /i/ /g/. Ask: What word?

5. Segmenting

Say cat, map, sit. Ask her to tell you all the sounds she hears.

6. Read words

Show: map, sat, tap, pin, sit, nap. No pictures. Ask her to read each word.

7. Read sentences

Show: Sam sat. Then: A cat sat. Ask her to read each sentence.
